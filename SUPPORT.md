# Getting help

- **How do I build my app for an older TV?** Read the
  [porting guide](https://github.com/Webos-Flutter-Legacy-TV/flutter-webos/blob/main/legacy-webos/docs/PORTING-GUIDE.md)
  and [legacy-webos/README.md](https://github.com/Webos-Flutter-Legacy-TV/flutter-webos/blob/main/legacy-webos/README.md).
- **Questions and ideas:** ask in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/Webos-Flutter-Legacy-TV/flutter-webos/discussions).
- **Something doesn't work:** search the issues in
  [flutter-webos](https://github.com/Webos-Flutter-Legacy-TV/flutter-webos/issues) and
  [plugins](https://github.com/Webos-Flutter-Legacy-TV/plugins/issues), then open a bug report.
- **Tell us about your TV:** the TV compatibility report form.
- **Problems with LG's SDK on webOS 26** belong upstream at
  [lg-flutter-webos](https://github.com/lg-flutter-webos).
